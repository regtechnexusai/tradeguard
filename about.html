About TradeGuard

TradeGuard is published by RegTech Nexus AI as an explainable, rule-based review-support workspace for trade-finance and transaction-monitoring workflows.

Product scope

The public build demonstrates two workspaces: TBML Check for trade evidence and transaction monitoring for early-warning patterns. It organises reviewer-entered or anonymised information, shows the reason for each configured flag and keeps human review required.

Public security boundary

  • Static public deployment on GitHub Pages with no login, backend, case database or server-side audit log.
  • Transaction-monitoring PDF text extraction is performed in the browser with the pinned self-hosted PDF.js 6.3.289 build at repository root; multiple PDFs can be analysed locally, but no TradeGuard upload endpoint is provided.
  • No live sanctions, PEP, adverse-media, market-price, bank, SWIFT or core-banking integration is included.
  • The public build is not audit-grade, institutionally certified or suitable for confidential customer data.

Institutional roadmap

A controlled pilot would require a custom domain and authenticated business email, identity and role access, encrypted server-side processing or an approved private deployment, case retention and audit controls, versioned rule governance, data-residency decisions, independent security testing and applicable regulatory review.
