HS Code is now mandatory. After a valid HS Code is verified, the Product /
commodity field is populated automatically from the tariff description.
Quantity, declared price, market range, origin and destination are also required.
For the price component, the benchmark source, source date, currency, unit of
measure and valuation basis are required. The expected unit profile is derived
from the verified HS Code for configured product groups, including HS 0206
edible offal on a weight basis. The unit selector is limited to the verified
profile where one is configured. If the HS Code, goods description, unit or
benchmark metadata is not comparable, the price component is withheld and the
overall risk grade is marked “Not decision-ready”.
If a mandatory field is empty or invalid, Submit shows a red border and an
inline error; after correction, the form can be submitted again.
Use “Start new case” to clear all previous transaction inputs and selections.
Route/port concerns require ports, business context and a documented route
rationale. Manual TBML indicator selections require confidence, evidence
status other than “Not provided”, and a reviewer evidence/rationale note.
The overall risk grade is withheld unless selected indicators have evidence
available for review; raw indicator points remain visible for QA purposes.
The form also includes a structured, multi-select TBML red-flag indicator
module. Selected indicators add explainable, non-duplicative review flags for
price/valuation, goods/HS Code, quantity/unit, documents, route/ports,
related-party/UBO, third-party payment, multiple/phantom shipment,
business-profile mismatch and unusual payment terms.
Use fictional or anonymised information only. Do not upload confidential LC,
invoice, passport, NID, bank or SWIFT documents to this public demo.
